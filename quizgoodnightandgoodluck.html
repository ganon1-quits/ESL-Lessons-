Introductory Overview 30 Minutes
Historical Context & Film Overview Set in 1950s America during the height of the Cold War and the anti-communist "Red Scare," Good Night, and Good Luck. (2005, dir. George Clooney) depicts the real-life conflict between legendary CBS broadcast journalist Edward R. Murrow, his producer Fred Friendly, and U.S. Senator Joseph McCarthy. As McCarthy uses fear, accusations, and political intimidation to silence opposition, Murrow and his See It Now news team decide to use the power of television journalism to expose his fear-mongering and protect civil liberties.
