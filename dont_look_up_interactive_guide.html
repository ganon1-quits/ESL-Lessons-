Introductory Segment: The Premise & Theme

Pre-Viewing Module
Movie Overview: Don't Look Up (2021) is a sharp political satire following two astronomers who discover an approaching comet capable of destroying Earth. As they try to warn the government and media, they are met with distraction, greed, and political division. The film explores themes of public communication, scientific consensus, media sensationalism, and political apathy.
Target Vocabulary
Satire (n.): The use of humor, exaggeration, or irony to expose and criticize political corruption or human foolishness.
Extinction (n.): The state or process of a species or group being completely wiped out.
Astronomer (n.): A scientist who studies space, celestial bodies, and the physics of the universe.
Whistleblower (n.): A person who exposes secretive, illegal, or unethical activity inside an organization to the public.
