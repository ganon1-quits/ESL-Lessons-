4. Global Fertility Rates

🔗 Read Article on The Guardian: Birthrates are plummeting worldwide. Can governments turn the tide?

Part 1: Key Vocabulary Definitions

  • Replacement (noun): The total fertility rate needed for a population to replace itself from one generation to the next (~2.1 births per woman).
  • Dependency (noun): A measure showing the ratio of dependents (under 15 or over 65) to the working-age population (15–64).
  • Incentive (noun): A financial or structural reward offered to encourage a person or group to take a specific action or behavior.
  • Contraction (noun): The process of becoming smaller, shrinking, or decreasing in economic size or volume.
