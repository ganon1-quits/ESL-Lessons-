Vocabulary Reference Glossary

Rhetoric (n.)
Persuasive speech or writing, often used in politics.
Mandate (v./n.)
To make something compulsory by official order or rule.
Misconception (n.)
A mistaken belief based on misunderstanding.
Civic engagement (n.phr.)
Active participation in community and public affairs.
Empower (v.)
To give someone confidence, resources, or authority.
Navigate (v.)
To manage or find one's way through a complex system.
Fuel (v.)
To motivate, nourish, or intensify a feeling or debate.
Detract from (phr.v.)
To diminish the value, quality, or importance of something.
